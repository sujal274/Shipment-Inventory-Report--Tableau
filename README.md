# 📦 Shipment & Inventory Analysis | Tableau

An interactive Tableau data analytics project focused on analyzing shipment performance, delivery delays, and inventory management. This project aims to help businesses understand logistics performance, identify operational challenges, and make data-driven decisions to improve supply chain efficiency.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Efficient shipment and inventory management are essential for smooth business operations. Delayed deliveries, inefficient shipping methods, and poor inventory planning can increase operational costs and affect customer satisfaction.

This project explores shipment and inventory data through interactive Tableau dashboards and a Tableau Story, presenting business insights in a clear and visual format.

The project is organized into two main sections:

* **Shipment Dashboard:** Analyzes shipment status, delivery delays, shipping methods, regional performance, and monthly trends.
* **Inventory Dashboard:** Focuses on stock availability, product-wise inventory, inventory trends, and potential stock management issues.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze shipment performance and delivery delays.
* Compare shipment delays across different shipping modes.
* Identify markets and countries experiencing delivery challenges.
* Track shipment delay trends over time.
* Monitor inventory levels and product availability.
* Identify potential stock shortages and excess inventory.
* Support better logistics and inventory planning through data visualization.

---

## 📊 Dashboard 1: Shipment Analysis

The Shipment Dashboard focuses on understanding shipment performance and identifying potential delivery problems.

### Key Analysis

* **Shipment Status:** Understand shipment status and delivery performance.
* **Shipment Delay Analysis:** Analyze average and median shipment delay days.
* **Delay Rate by Market:** Compare delivery delays across different markets.
* **Delay Rate by Shipment Mode:** Evaluate delays across different shipping methods.
* **Geographic Analysis:** Investigate shipment delays across countries.
* **Monthly Shipment Trends:** Monitor changes in shipment delays over time.
* **Delayed Order Items:** Examine the percentage of delayed order items.

### Business Value

This dashboard helps identify areas that require further investigation, compare shipping methods, and understand regional delivery challenges. These insights can support better logistics planning and improve shipment efficiency.

---

## 📦 Dashboard 2: Inventory Analysis

The Inventory Dashboard is designed to analyze stock availability, inventory distribution, and product-level inventory performance.

### Key Analysis

* **Inventory Overview:** Understand the overall inventory position.
* **Product-Wise Inventory:** Compare inventory levels across products.
* **Stock Availability:** Identify products with potentially low or excessive stock.
* **Inventory Trends:** Track inventory changes over time.
* **Inventory Cost Analysis:** Examine inventory costs to support cost management.
* **Warehouse Analysis:** Evaluate inventory distribution across warehouses, where the necessary data is available.

### Business Value

This dashboard helps businesses monitor stock levels, improve inventory planning, and identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary inventory costs. It supports more informed purchasing, replenishment, and stock allocation decisions.

## 📖 Tableau Story

The project uses a Tableau Story to present the analysis in two main sections.

### 1. Shipment Dashboard Story

Explores shipment performance, delivery delays, shipping methods, regional differences, and monthly trends to identify logistics challenges and opportunities for improving delivery efficiency.

### 2. Inventory Dashboard Story

Explores inventory levels, product availability, inventory costs, and stock trends to identify potential shortages or excess inventory and support better inventory management.

The story connects dashboard findings with business questions to make the analysis easier to understand.
